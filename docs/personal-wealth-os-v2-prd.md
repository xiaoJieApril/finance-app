# Personal Wealth OS v2.0 PRD

## Product Positioning

This app is no longer only a bookkeeping app. It is a Personal Wealth OS for managing cash flow, assets, investments, goals, budgets, and AI financial reviews.

## Implemented Foundation

- Dashboard: total assets, liabilities, net worth, monthly income, monthly expenses, monthly savings, investment value, emergency fund, savings rate, investment rate, and financial score.
- Asset accounts: supports cash, bank, e-wallet, credit card, money market, stock, ETF, crypto, retirement, fixed deposit, investment, and other account types.
- Asset allocation: derives allocation from account balances and groups it into cash, bank, money market, stocks, ETF, crypto, investment, and others.
- Spending insights: detects top category, highest spending day, average daily spending, weekend spending, possible subscriptions, and duplicate purchases.
- AI context: monthly/weekly AI review receives wealth, allocation, goals, budgets, recurring items, and spending insights.

## Next Roadmap

- Dedicated Net Worth page with monthly snapshots, yearly growth, highest net worth, and lowest net worth.
- Dedicated Investment Tracker with purchase price, quantity, current price, market value, profit, and ROI.
- Transaction tags with filtering and tag-based insights.
- Financial calendar for salary day, bills, investment day, goal deposit, and upcoming payments.
- Backup and sync flows for JSON/CSV import/export, with Google Drive and iCloud reserved for future integration.
- AI Settings screen with secure local storage and provider abstraction for Gemini, OpenAI, and Claude.
